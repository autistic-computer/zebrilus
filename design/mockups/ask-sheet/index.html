Question sheet

Two questions about the sheet a question grows out of the composer. One: what takes the place of the "Question" header. Two: how a multi-select answer shows it is picked without a checkbox. The first figure in each row is what ships today. The highlighted row is where the keyboard is.

1. The header

Today the sheet spends a row on an icon and the word "Question", then a caption with the question's short label, then the question. The short label is what the agent gives each question as a tab name ("Accent", "Scope"), 12 characters at most.

2. Picked, without a checkbox

Each figure shows the same state: "Questions" and "Tool approvals" are picked, the keyboard is on "Tool approvals", "Plan approval" and "Subagent prompts" are not picked. The cue has to hold in all four combinations and must not move the text when it appears.

3. Together

Header C with cue A, at the width the sheet has in a pane. Left: one question on its own, where there is nothing to step through, so the sheet opens straight on the question. Right: the second of three, a pick-any, with one answer already given.
