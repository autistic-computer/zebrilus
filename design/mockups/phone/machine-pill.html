Machine pill

Take A, reworked. The tiles and the capsule are 48 now, 4 from the pill's edge instead of 8, so the name and the session line sit 5 off the capsule's top and bottom instead of 1. Inside, the mark is centred on a 48 circle (14 in), the text starts 10 after it, and the capsule is as wide as its text needs plus 16, so names are not cut. Then what happens with more machines than fit: A1 scrolls the row, A2 folds the rest into a count. In the browser the phones play the swipe; the strips are still frames.

A. The tile grows

The machine on screen stretches its tile into a capsule with its name over the session in view; the others are their marks on 48 tiles, 4 apart. When there is room, the other of two machines keeps its name beside its tile. Swiping, the capsule hands its width to the next tile as the finger moves; its text is gone by the middle of the swipe and the next one's comes in after it, so no name is ever cut short. A long name can push the third tile under the edge fade, as in A1.

A1. More machines: the tiles scroll

Picked. The capsule keeps its width and every tile its 48, so once the row is wider than the pill it scrolls to keep the capsule in the middle, clamped at the two ends, and a 20 fade marks the side where more machines are. A swipe scrolls the row as the capsule moves, so the next machine always shows.

A2. More machines: the rest fold into a count

Not picked. From four machines the pill shows the one on screen, the next one, and a "+n" tile for the rest. A tap on the count opens Machines. A swipe trades the capsule with the next tile as in A; once it lands, the tile after it takes the free place and the count stays. The capsule gets what is left, 130, so a long name is cut.
