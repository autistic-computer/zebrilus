The pill

The pill is the phone's one control, and its best move, dragging it up to zoom out, is the one nothing on screen tells you about. Three takes on making the pill show what it does and do more, all drawn on the same moment: a terminal that just ran the tests in zebrilus, with a Claude chat one pane over. A and C can ship together; B changes where the drag stops.

A. The handle

The smallest change. The drag gets a visible twin, and the pill says where the drag is going while you drag.

A, with the pane going home

The lift done the way a phone app goes home. Today the whole machine page zooms as one camera move on a single number, so the pane cannot follow the finger and the page swims under it. Here the page stands still, the pane comes loose as a card that rides the finger, and letting go throws it into its own preview.

ArmedBuilt at the lift's existing numbers: travel past 100 (a third of its 300), or a flick up faster than 500 a second. Pill at full ink, disc inverted, one tick. Dropping back under disarms. B. The switcher

The drag gets a first stop. A short pull shows the panes as cards, so paging is something you see; a long pull or a flick goes on to the overview as today.

C. The live pill

The pill works between drags. What needs you in another pane docks beside it, and can be answered without leaving the pane you are in.
