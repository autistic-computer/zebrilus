A machine

Step 2 of the phone redesign, after Machines: the screen inside a machine. Every level is the same screen: a large title, a two-column grid of tiles built like the machine tile, the add tile last, the whole tile the target, a zoom to go in. Here the tiles are windows, grouped by session. A window tile says in words what the window is doing, with the desktop's own words and tones, and holds no live preview: a phone shows one pane at a time, and 4 px text says nothing. Holding any tile, or a session's header, opens a sheet of what you can do to it. If the connection drops, the screen zooms back out to the machine's tile, which says Not connected. Every frame is built from the phone components. Caption text in italics says what the native app does today where it differs; today the screen stays and says Connection lost. 3. Window tile states

One tile per state, with the desktop chat list's words and tones (window_summary.rs:464-554). Waiting for you is the warning dot and its words; failed and an exit are the danger dot; done and not yet seen is a dot in the ink, with Done in the ink, since the accent is a person's pick and Yaru's orange would read as waiting; working is the spinner and the work's own message, which shortens before its percent does. Idle says nothing. Working is never green: green only means a machine is connected. The foot's glyphs say what the window holds, then the folder and branch, or a page's host, and the age of the last change. Today the phone reads none of this but the warning dot, though every pane carries its folder, repo, branch and last change (snapshot.rs:504).
