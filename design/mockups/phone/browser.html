A browser

Step 5 of the phone redesign, after Machines, A machine, A pane and A terminal: the browser pane. It follows the rule every pane follows, no header: the page gets the whole screen and the pill is the browser's bar. Its stack menu, from Menus, leads with Back, Forward and Reload in one row nearest the thumb, so Back is one stroke from the pill. The page stays the phone's own web view, with its own scrolling, taps and keyboard. Caption text in italics says what the native app does today where it differs.
