Machines

The app opens on the machines overview, and it holds only machines you added. With nothing added it is the title, the add tile and the gear: that is the first run, no welcome page. Going anywhere is a zoom: machines, then a machine, then a pane, each step growing the tile you tapped and shrinking back the way you came. Adding a machine is the same move into the add tile, and machines found on your tailnet wait there as rows, not on the overview. Only a short question, like the ssh password, is a sheet. Every frame is built from the phone components. Caption text in italics says what the native app does today where it differs. Next, inside a machine: A machine.
