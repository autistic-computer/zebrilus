Machines

The app's root is the machines overview, the bento grid today's All hosts already is. With nothing saved it is the same grid with one tile. The zoom is the navigation: machines, then a machine, then a pane, each step growing the tile you tapped and shrinking back the way you came. Adding a machine is the same move into the add tile. On a tailnet there is nothing to add: the machines are already in the grid. One way in per thing, no lists, no extra sheets. Caption text in this ink marks what the phone can't do today.
