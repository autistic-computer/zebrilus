Pill dots

On the split bar from The bar, worked through, three takes on what replaces the page dots. Same moment: zebrilus has five panes in four windows (cargo test and a waiting chat side by side, a second chat, the board in a browser, zsh), and the terminal is on screen.

Up close

The bar at one and a half times, so the small parts read.

A. Glyph dots

Each dot becomes the pane's glyph at 12, still grouped by window. You see that a chat is next without going there.

B. Neighbour names

No dots. The line under the title says where a swipe goes: the next pane's name with an arrow, and where you are as a count.

C. Scrubber

The dots become a thin strip, one segment per window, a cell per pane. Hold the pill and slide to scrub: every pane rises above the bar as a card, and letting go jumps to the one under the finger.
