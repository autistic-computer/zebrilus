Menus

One menu for the whole phone: the stack menu. Chips rise in a column out of the thing you pressed, the pill or a key in the key row, nearest first, the most used nearest the thumb. Tap the anchor and the stack stays for a tap on a chip. Or hold it, slide up and let go on a chip, in one stroke. The pill's menu in A machine and A pane and the chords in A terminal are stack menus; the arrow pad keeps its bubble, drawn as a sibling. A browser adds a row of glyph chips for Back, Forward and Reload.
