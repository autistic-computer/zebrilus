Bar tweaks

Two questions about the bar. Closing a menu: today every chip slides toward the button it came from, shrinks to half and fades on a spring with a long tail, so for about 100ms a cluster of tiny faint chips sits over the button. The same close runs on every stack menu (the plus menu, the pill's menu, the browser's, a key's chords). Four replacements, each shown as frames from the tap on the close and, in the browser, as a replay. Going up: the up disc's chevron reads as "scroll up". One take keeps the disc with a diagonal arrow; two drop it and mark the pill as something you drag up, drawn on a pane's bar and a machine's.

Today: fold into the button

Each chip moves toward the button's centre, shrinks to half and fades, all on one spring. The spring's tail is what leaves the ghost cluster.

Picked. The chips, their labels and the scrim fade out together in 120ms, in place. Nothing moves; the close turns back into the plus in the same 120ms. The fastest that still reads as a close.

The pill, fixed

Built. A machine that is not on screen is its mark alone, no circle under it. The capsule takes whatever the row leaves, so the row ends where the pill ends and there is no gap at the right. When the row needs more than the pill, the machines next to the capsule stay whole, the next one past each cut edge shows half its mark under a 28 fade from the pill's glass, and the capsule takes the slack so the cut always halves a mark. Drawn without the up disc, as U2 would leave it.
