A pane

Step 3 of the phone redesign, after Machines and A machine: inside a pane. First the chrome every pane shares, then the agent chat. A pane has no header, as today: its content runs to the edges and the pill at the bottom is the one handle, saying what the pane is and what state it is in, the way a window tile does. When the keyboard comes up the pill gives its place to what you type with. The chat is the desktop's chat drawn for a thumb: one composer row with a plus and a send, a card in the composer's place when the agent asks, and nothing small under the messages. The pill's menu is a stack menu, drawn and live with every gesture on Menus. A terminal is the next step, and the browser gets its own; they appear here only where a shared rule needs them. Caption text in italics says what the native app does today where it differs.
