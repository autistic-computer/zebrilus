The bar

Today the bottom of the phone is one pill: it names the pane, pages on a swipe, goes up on its disc or a drag, and hides everything else behind a tap. Three takes on breaking it into pieces that each do one thing, all on the same moment: a terminal in zebrilus that just ran the tests, with a Claude chat one pane over waiting to run a command. Every take carries the same attention disc, which shows only while something waits on you.

Today

1. Split bar

The same parts pulled apart. Up leaves the pill for a disc of its own on the left, and making something new gets a disc on the right, since it is what the menu is opened for most.

2. Pane dock

The dots become the panes. Every pane in the session is a glyph in the bar, grouped by window, and the one on screen widens to say its name. Jumping is a tap, not a count of swipes.

3. Context bar

The pill stays, and beside it go the one or two things you do most in that kind of pane. The discs change with the pane; the pill and its gestures do not.
