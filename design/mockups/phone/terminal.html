A terminal

Step 4 of the phone redesign, after Machines, A machine and A pane: the terminal pane. Its menus, the pill's and the chords behind ctrl and alt, are stack menus, drawn and live on Menus. It keeps what works today, a terminal to the edges, the pill under it and a key row over the keyboard, and fixes sizes and consistency around it: 44 keys, the ends of the row always in view, and a tap, a hold or a sheet for what the desktop does with a pointer. New on the phone: a sheet for links, find, and jump to bottom. Caption text in italics says what the native app does today where it differs.
