Arrow pad

Today holding the arrow disc pops a 152 bubble with a 3 by 3 grid over the row; dragging picks a cell and holding it repeats. Four takes that keep everything in the disc. All of them tick on each arrow sent (selection_haptic), pick a direction once the finger has moved 10, and stop the moment the finger lifts. The live strip on each take takes a mouse drag on the disc and moves the caret in the line above.

A1. Ticks only

No popup and nothing moves. While held, the disc's glyph turns into four small chevrons around a dot; the one the finger points at grows and takes the ink. Holding repeats after 300ms, then every 80ms, a tick per arrow. The least to draw, the least to see.

A2. Thumbstick

The disc's inner 48 knob follows the finger, held to 10 from the middle, like a thumbstick; the knob lights the wash and its chevron points where it leans. Repeat speeds up the further you lean: at 10 it is 300ms then 80ms, and it eases back to the middle on release with the touch spring.

A3. Trackpad

No timer: every 14 of travel sends one arrow, so the caret moves as fast as the finger does, and reversing goes back. A faint trail grows from the disc along the drag and the glyph's chevron lights. Precise for walking a cursor through a command line; a long jump means a long drag.

A4. Bloom

The disc grows in place to 96, centred on itself, the four arrows on its rim and the finger's one in the wash; it is the old bubble shrunk into the button instead of floating above the row. Repeat as today.
